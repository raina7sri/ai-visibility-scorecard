What It Measures

Six metrics you score from what you actually see in each assistant's answer.

  • Mention Rate — % of scored responses where your brand is named at all.
  • Recommendation Rate — % where your brand is actively recommended, not merely listed.
  • Share of Voice — your mentions ÷ (your + competitor) mentions.
  • Citation Rate — % of complete, valid answer observations that cite your website; also reported conditionally among brand mentions.
  • Accuracy — complete responses classified fully correct, out of accuracy-scored responses (optional; needs ground truth). Partial and wrong remain separate.
  • Average Position — mean rank when you're listed among options (lower is better).

Why Score by Hand? The developer APIs behind these models don't behave like the consumer versions of ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity, which search the web and cite sources live when your customers actually use them. Therefore, the accurate way to measure what a real buyer sees is to read the actual answer and score it. This tool makes that fast, easy, and consistent to do for any new or existing product, service, or company.
